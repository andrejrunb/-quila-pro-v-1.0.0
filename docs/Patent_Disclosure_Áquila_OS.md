# Patent Disclosure: Áquila Pro - Autonomous School OS

**Title**: Systems and Methods for Autonomous Institutional Management via Multi-Agent Swarm Intelligence.

## 1. Field of Invention
This invention relates to the field of Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIEd) and IoT-driven building management. Specifically, it addresses the technical problem of institutional fragmentation and data-lag in educational environments.

## 2. Background & Problem Statement
Traditional schools suffer from "Data Latency"—the gap between an event (e.g., student disengagement, financial deficit, or security breach) and the institutional response. Current ERPs are passive repositories.

## 3. Summary of the Invention (The Novelty)
The invention comprises an **Autonomous School Operating System** (Áquila OS) characterized by a decentralized swarm of agents that possess "Environmental Consciousness."

### Key Innovative Claims:
1. **Affective Re-ID Continuity**: A system that maintains student identity across non-overlapping camera fields of view by combining gait analysis with micro-expression (affective) profiling for educational engagement scoring.
2. **Autonomous Financial Reconciliation (Bayesian matching)**: A method for instantly matching PIX payment signatures to student ledgers without human intervention, triggering autonomous debt-negotiation agents.
3. **Bio-Safety/Energy Signal-to-Actuator Bridge**: A feedback loop where air quality and occupancy heatmaps are translated into immediate physical actuator commands (HVAC/Windows) to optimize cognitive performance.
4. **Institutional Legacy Bridge (SHA-256 Ingestion)**: A cryptographic method for verifying and ingesting fragmented legacy SIS data into a unified "Institutional Memory."

## 4. Detailed Description of the Swarm Logic
Unlike centralized systems, the claimed OS utilizes "Squad Spawning." For example, when a threshold of CO2 is reached, the `InfrastructureAgent` does not just notify; it spawns an `EnvironmentSquad` that interfaces with hardware relays.

## 5. Utility & Industrial Application
- **Zero-Typing Classrooms**: Reducing teacher burnout by 40% through automated diarization.
- **Autonomous Safe-Schools**: Replacing third-party monitoring contracts with 24/7 AI perimeters.
- **Real-Time Sustainability**: Reducing carbon footprint through intelligent zone-based power curfews.

## 6. Non-Obviousness
Combining affective computing with institutional security protocols and financial automation in a single, decentralized swarm is a significant technical advancement over current centralized, user-input-dependent systems.
