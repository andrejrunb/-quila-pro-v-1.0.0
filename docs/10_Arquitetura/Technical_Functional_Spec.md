## 1. Architectural Overview: The Swarm
Áquila Pro is built on a **Decentralized Multi-Agent Swarm** model. It does not rely on a single central server for logic; instead, specialized agents cooperate to maintain institutional equilibrium.

### A. Core Agent Roles
- **TeacherAgent**: Automates the 'Zero-Typing Classroom'. Handles diarization, photo-to-diary conversion, and student emotional profiling.
- **SecurityAgent**: Manages the physical perimeter. Spawns `PerimeterWatchdog` and `NightSentry` squads.
- **SecretaryAgent**: Financial heart. Manages `ReconciliationSquad` (PIX/ERP) and automated debt recovery.
- **InfrastructureAgent**: The skeletal guardian. Manages self-healing hardware, Bio-Safety (Air quality), and Energy Sovereignty.
- **AdminAgent**: Global auditor. Ensures WASM integrity and manages Legacy Sync.

## 2. Technical Services Stack

### I. Vision & Affective Engine
- **Affective Re-ID**: Tracking student movement (gait/posture) while identifying micro-expressions (engagement vs. boredom).
- **RuView Integration**: Edge-AI processing for real-time occupancy heatmaps.

### II. Financial Sovereignty
- **PIX Reconciliation**: Real-time matching of instant payments to student IDs without human oversight.
- **Financial Pulse**: A Bayesian forecasting engine that predicts cashflow health based on historical payment patterns.

### III. Sustainability & Bio-Safety
- **BioSafetyAutomator**: Signal-to-Actuator logic that opens windows or adjusts HVAC based on PM2.5 and CO2 levels.
- **Energy Curfews**: Zone-based power throttling driven by occupancy heatmaps, resulting in ~25% energy savings.

## 3. The IntegrationHUB
An agnostic layer that bridges legacy systems (Urania, Siescola) to the Swarm.
- **LegacySovereigntyBridge**: SHA-256 verified ingestion of historical records.
- **External API Bridge**: Secure, encrypted tunnels for reporting alerts to local authorities.

## 4. UI/UX: The Evolution Dashboard
- **Glassmorphic Interface**: High-contrast, premium design inspired by modern SOC (Security Operations Center) layouts.
- **Role-Based Portals**: Tailored experiences for Parents (Elo), Teachers, Students (Portfolio), and Directors (Ecosystem Intelligence).

## 5. Security Protocols
- **Institutional Guard**: Auto-switching between `ACADEMIC_DAY`, `NIGHT_SENTRY`, and `EVENT_PROTOCOL`.
